$a$ & Age class indicator\\
$a_{50}$ & age at 50\% of full selectivity by a survey or the fishery\\
$A$ & Last age class (``plus group'')\\
$f$ & Fishing fleet indicator\\
$g(\theta_j, \theta_j)$ & either relative error or Mohn's $\rho$ of estimation of $\theta_j$\\
$F$ & Fully-selected fishing mortality rate\\
$F_{40\%}$ & Fully-selected fishing mortality rate reducing spawing biomass per recruit to 40\% of the unfished value\\
$F_{\text{MSY}}$ & Fully-selected fishing mortality rate providing maximum sustainable yield\\
$j$ & Indicator for a simulated population and set of observations\\
$k$ & Parameter in von Bertalanffy growth model\\
$l_m$ & lower bound for catchability of index $m$\\
$l_s$ & lower bound for selectivity parameter $s$\\
$L_a$ & Length at age $a$ in von Bertalanffy growth model\\
$L_\infty$ & Maximum Length in von Bertalanffy growth model\\
$\log\text{SD}_{\text{SSB}}$ & log-standard deviation of the true log(SSB)\\
$m$ & Indicator for indices of abundance\\
$M_{a,y}$ & Natural mortality rate at age $a$ in year $y$\\
$N_{a,y}$ & Population number at age $a$ in year $y$\\
$p_{s,y}$ & Selectivity parameter $s$\\
$p$ & indicator of retrospective peels\\
$P$ & Total number of retrospective peels\\
$\text{SSB}_{y}$ & Spawning stock biomass in year $y$\\
$t_0$ & Age at length equal to 0 in von Bertalanffy growth model\\
$u_m$ & upper bound for catchability of index $m$\\
$u_s$ & upper bound for selectivity parameter $s$\\
$W_a$ & Weight at age $a$\\
$x_j$  & the transformed relative error or Mohn's $\rho$ value used in analyses\\
$y$ & Year indicator\\
$Y$ & Last year of simulated populations and data\\
$\alpha$ & Beverton-Holt parameter representing pre-recruit density-independent mortality\\
$\beta$ & Beverton-Holt parameter representing pre-recruit density-dependent mortality\\
$\epsilon_{m,y}$ & Logit-scale random effect for year $y$ of catchability of index $m$\\
$\epsilon_{M,y}$ & Log-scale natural mortality rate random effect for year $y$\\
$\epsilon_{s,y}$ & Logit-scale random effect for selectivity parameter $s$ in year $y$\\
$\kappa_1$ & Scalar parameter of allometric length-weight relationship\\
$\kappa_2$ & Power parameter of allometric length-weight relationship\\
$\mu_m$ & Temporal mean (or constant) logit-scale catchability of index $m$\\
$\mu_M$ & Temporal mean (or constant) log-scale natural mortality rate\\
$\mu_{s}$ & Logit-scale mean for selectivity parameter $s$\\
$\theta$ & Model parameter or derived variable\\
$\widehat \theta$ & Estimate of parameter or derived variable $\theta$\\
$\rho_m$ & auto-regressive correlation parameter for logit-scale random effects for catchabilit of index $m$\\
$\rho_M$ & auto-regressive correlation parameter for log-scale natural mortality rate random effects\\
$\sigma_s$ & marginal standard deviation of logit-scale random effects for selectivity parameter $s$\\
$\sigma_m$ & marginal standard deviation of logit-scale random effects for catchability of index $m$\\
$\sigma_M$ & marginal standard deviation of log-scale natural mortality rate random effects\\
$\sigma_R$ & marginal standard deviation of log-scale recruitment random effects\\
$\sigma_{2+}$ & marginal standard deviation of log-scale apparent survival random effects\\
